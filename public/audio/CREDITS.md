# Audio credits

Bank folders hold one loop per recorded RPM, named `<rpm>.wav` (44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono).

## engine-sim recordings — Stunt Rally 3

Recorded by **CryHam** in [engine-sim](https://github.com/ange-yaghi/engine-sim) at steady RPM, EQ'd in Audacity.
Source: [stuntrally/stuntrally3 `data/sounds/engines`](https://github.com/stuntrally/stuntrally3/tree/main/data/sounds/engines).
License: [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Files copied unmodified, renamed `<series>-<n>.wav` → `<n>000.wav`.

| Folder | Series | Engine (identified by firing-order spectrum) |
|---|---|---|
| `i4_tsu` | `tsu-1..8` | 4-cylinder |
| `v6_tsp` | `tsp-1..8` | even-fire 6-cylinder |
| `v8_gv8` | `gv8-1..7` | cross-plane V8 |
